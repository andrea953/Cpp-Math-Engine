name: 2027 CPP and plan limits
description: Verified CRA annual ceilings and the distinction between enacted and in-force CPP rate changes.
---

CRA's registered-plan limits table confirms the following annual values:

- YMPE: $76,700, up from $74,600.
- YAMPE: $87,400, up from $85,000.
- Basic exemption: $3,500, unchanged.
- RRSP dollar limit: $35,390 for 2027 and $36,390 for 2028.
- 2027 money purchase limit: $36,390; defined benefit limit: $4,043.33; DPSP limit: $18,195.

The user describes the 2027 rate reduction as final. However, the Justice Laws amendments-not-in-force page (modified November 2, 2026) still lists the relevant 2026, c.22 amendments. Bill C-30 section 44(2) requires a commencement order. Recheck official commencement and CRA payroll guidance before treating the reduction as unconditional.

The proposed/enacted schedule lowers the actual base contribution rate from 4.95% to 4.75% per employee/employer. Including the unchanged 1% first additional contribution gives 5.95% to 5.75%; self-employed combined rates are 11.90% to 11.50%. CPP2 remains 4% per side.

**Why:** Annual ceilings are published separately from contribution-rate commencement; combining them can incorrectly turn a conditional rate change into an official payroll rule. Contribution rates are also distinct from pension replacement/accrual rates.

**How to apply:** Use verified annual ceilings, retain the user's CPP-calculations-and-wording scope, distinguish base from combined rates, and keep registered-plan limits tied to their tax years.
