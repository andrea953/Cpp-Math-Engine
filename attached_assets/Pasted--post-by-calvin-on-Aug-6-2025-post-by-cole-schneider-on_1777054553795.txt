To clarify for others who may come to this thread, you can set an “Indexing Rate” for CPP & OAS on the Government Benefits page, which is essentially both the YMPE growth rate (that is what CPP grows at before you start CPP) and the Indexing Rate (that is what CPP grows at after you start it).

You can’t set a separate YMPE rate, which is unfortunate :frowning: .
